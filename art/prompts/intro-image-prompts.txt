FIVE BLADES - IMAGE PROMPTS FOR THE OPENING CUTSCENES
=====================================================

One image per card of each protagonist's opening (apps/app/src/story/prelude.ts).
Save each image under the file name given above its prompt, as .webp or .png, in:

    apps/app/src/story/intro/

The cutscene shows an image behind its card when a file of that name is there, and the plain card when it is not, so
they can be added a few at a time.

Size: 1920 x 1080 (16:9). Keep the lower third quiet and dark: the card's words are set over it.


SHARED STYLE - paste this after every prompt
--------------------------------------------
High-detail painterly pixel art, dark fantasy, cinematic wide shot, 16:9. Deep shadows, embers and drifting ash,
warm torchlight against cold night, volumetric light, rich saturated accents on a dark ground. Same look as the
game's title art: small hooded swordsmen with faces lost in shadow and two glowing eyes, katanas worn across the
back, long ragged cloaks. Lower third of the frame dark and uncluttered. No text, no letters, no logos, no
watermark, no UI.

The five hooded swordsmen, by protagonist (keep the colours exact so they read as the same characters):
  The Forbidden  - black cloak with gold trim, gold eyes, a huge executioner's greatsword
  The Ember      - crimson cloak, orange-red eyes, a furnace-forged greatsword glowing at the edge
  The Architect  - deep blue cloak, ice-blue eyes, several enchanted blades floating around him
  The Exile      - violet cloak, white-violet eyes, two swords (one long, one short), sparks of lightning
  The Hollow     - green cloak, pale green eyes, a thin ritual blade trailing ghostly light

The Reaper: a tall gaunt hooded figure in a tattered black robe, a long scythe, two small red eyes in the dark of the
hood, never shown attacking anyone; he watches.

SHARED CARDS (shown before every protagonist's story)
=====================================================

prelude-vareth.webp
  "In Vareth, power is never made. It is taken: from blood, from relics, from bargains, and from the dead."
PROMPT: A vast dark kingdom seen from a cliff at dusk: a black-walled crown city with spires, a mountain range under
snow to the north, a glowing volcanic reach to the east, drowned marshland in mist to the south. Thin streams of
pale light rise from graveyards and battlefields across the land and are drawn toward the city's towers like smoke
into a chimney. A broken crown half buried in the foreground rock. Ominous, epic, quiet.

prelude-reaper-1.webp
  "A hooded figure will walk beside you. He is the Reaper. He comes for the dead..."
PROMPT: The Reaper standing alone on an old stone battlefield at night, scythe resting against his shoulder, his
robe moving in a wind that touches nothing else. Around his feet, faint souls rise from the fallen like lantern
light and drift into his open hand. Ancient, patient, not threatening. Moonlight, mist, a ruined banner.

prelude-reaper-2.webp
  "Lately the dead arrive with nothing left in them, or do not arrive at all..."
PROMPT: The Reaper kneeling over a fallen soldier in a ruined street, reaching for a soul that is not there: the
body gives off only a thin grey husk of light that crumbles away. Far behind him, a stream of stolen soul-light is
being pulled into the sky toward an unseen source. His red eyes turned toward it. Unease, mystery, cold blue light.

THE FORBIDDEN - "The Stolen God"   (black and gold)
=====================================================

prelude-forbidden-1.webp
  "You are the Crown's executioner. For fourteen years you have ended those whose magic was too dangerous to cage..."
PROMPT: The Forbidden in the black-and-gold hooded cloak, standing on an execution platform inside a grim stone
prison courtyard at night, the huge greatsword planted point-down before him, both hands on the hilt. Runes along the
blade glow faintly gold. Rows of iron cages behind, chained sorcerers glowing faintly inside. Rain, torches,
Blackspire's black towers above.

prelude-forbidden-2.webp
  "Lord Commander Veylan is the Crown's chief magical executioner..."
PROMPT: Lord Commander Veylan: a tall, severe, grey-haired knight in immaculate black Crown plate with a high collar
and a long black cape, standing at a training yard rail with hands clasped behind his back, watching a young recruit
in a black-and-gold hood practise sword forms below. Cold dawn light, banners of the Crown, disciplined and austere.

prelude-forbidden-3.webp
  "Veylan believes one person's freedom is a small price for a civilization that survives..."
PROMPT: Veylan seen from below on a palace balcony above a vast orderly city at night, every street lit in neat
lines. He looks down at the city, not at the viewer, with a calm, almost sad expression. In the street far below, a
tiny figure in a black-and-gold hood looks back up at him. Scale, distance, cold certainty.

prelude-forbidden-4.webp
  "He came to Blackspire tonight for an old prisoner. He will leave without him, and follow your sword instead."
PROMPT: Inside a narrow prison cell lit by a single candle: an elderly prisoner in rags with glowing runes on his
skin sits against the wall. In the doorway stands the Forbidden with the greatsword. In the darkest corner behind
the old man, half seen, the Reaper waits, his red eyes on the Forbidden's sword, not the prisoner.

prelude-forbidden-5.webp
  "It begins at Blackspire, with one last sentence."
PROMPT: Blackspire prison from outside: a black fortress on a crag in a thunderstorm, one high window lit gold. The
Forbidden stands small on the cliff path in the foreground, cloak streaming, greatsword on his back, looking up at
the window. Lightning, rain, the moment before everything changes.

THE EMBER - "The Saint of Ash"   (crimson)
=====================================================

prelude-ember-1.webp
  "You are from Cinderfall, a town the Crown is about to end..."
PROMPT: The Ember in the crimson hooded cloak on a rooftop of a soot-black mining town at the foot of a volcano.
Crown soldiers with torches march into the streets below; townsfolk huddle in doorways. Old painted posters of a
saint peel off the walls around her. Her greatsword glows red at its edge. Orange sky, smoke, tension.

prelude-ember-2.webp
  "Under the Ashen Reach lies the Furnace Below. For generations it has fed on the memories of the dead..."
PROMPT: An enormous cavern deep under a volcano: a living furnace of molten rock and fire with a single vast glowing
eye-like core at its heart. Faint ghostly figures of the dead drift down from cracks in the ceiling and dissolve into
the flames, their memories rising as sparks. Awe, heat haze, red and gold.

prelude-ember-3.webp
  "It does not want to destroy anyone. It wants to know what being alive is like..."
PROMPT: A burning town at night. Out of a collapsing building comes the Ember, carried on a rising wave of living
fire shaped like great gentle hands. Her eyes blaze. The flames around her look curious rather than cruel, lit from
inside like glass. Ruins, embers, a sense of something vast tasting the world through her.

prelude-ember-4.webp
  "He has been busy in Cinderfall since dusk, and the count is already wrong..."
PROMPT: The Reaper walking a street of Cinderfall in flames, counting: a row of the fallen on either side, most of
them giving off no soul-light at all. He holds a long knotted cord, tying knots for the dead, and the cord is too
short. Fire behind, ash falling like snow, his red eyes troubled.

prelude-ember-5.webp
  "It begins on the last night Cinderfall stands."
PROMPT: Cinderfall seen from the volcano's slope at night, the whole town burning beneath a red sky, smoke rising
into a column lit from below. In the foreground the Ember stands with her back to us, greatsword dragging a line of
fire in the rock, her crimson cloak torn and smoking.

THE EXILE - "The Nine Bells"   (violet)
=====================================================

prelude-exile-1.webp
  "You are the best student of the Nine Bells, the oldest school of the blade in the Pale North..."
PROMPT: A mountain monastery-school carved into a snowy cliff, nine great bronze bells hanging in a row under a
wooden gate. In the frozen courtyard the Exile in the violet hooded cloak practises with two swords, one long and one
short; violet lightning trails his blades. Other hooded students watch in a ring. Snow, pale blue dawn.

prelude-exile-2.webp
  "The Bellkeeper leads the Nine Bells. He raised you, corrected your stance..."
PROMPT: The Bellkeeper: an old swordmaster in grey robes with a long white beard and a lantern on a staff, gently
adjusting the guard of a small violet-hooded child holding a wooden sword. Warm lantern light inside a wooden
training hall, snow at the window. Tender, fatherly, quietly sad.

prelude-exile-3.webp
  "He knows the Great Binding that holds the old powers down is failing. Every year he gives his nine best students..."
PROMPT: A colossal stone seal carved into the face of a mountain, cracked, with chains of light holding something
vast and dark beneath it. Nine small hooded figures walk single file up a stair toward it through snow, each carrying
a lit lantern. The Bellkeeper watches from below with his head bowed. Cold, solemn, terrible.

prelude-exile-4.webp
  "Nine are given to the mountain every year, and none of the nine has ever reached him."
PROMPT: The Reaper waiting alone at the top of a snowy mountain stair beside the great cracked seal, scythe in hand,
nine empty places in the snow before him where nine should have arrived. Behind him, nine faint lights are being
pulled sideways into a crack in the stone instead. Blizzard, isolation, mystery.

prelude-exile-5.webp
  "It begins with the last examination, and nine bells."
PROMPT: The nine great bells of the school swinging and ringing in a storm, violet lightning striking the cliff.
Below them, the Exile stands with both swords drawn, a parchment roll pinned to the gate beside him with nine names
on it, the last one glowing. Dramatic low angle, snow whipping past.

THE HOLLOW - "The Dead Remember"   (green)
=====================================================

prelude-hollow-1.webp
  "You are a healer of the Drowned Wilds. You died among children you could not save..."
PROMPT: A sunken village in a misty swamp, stilt houses half under black water, glowing marsh lights. The Hollow in
the green hooded cloak floats upright just above the water, newly risen, her eyes opening with pale green light, a
thin ritual blade in her hand trailing spectral light. Small ghostly handprints glow on her cloak. Eerie, sorrowful.

prelude-hollow-2.webp
  "Between memory and death there is the Witness. It is not cruel. It does not know what cruelty is."
PROMPT: A boundless dim space between life and death filled with floating fragments of memories like shards of glass,
each showing a small moving scene. At the centre, the Witness: a huge, faceless, gentle presence made of pale light
and countless watching eyes that look kind rather than menacing, its many hands cradling the shards. Silent, vast.

prelude-hollow-3.webp
  "It believes memories are the only things that give existence meaning, and it wants every human life kept, for ever..."
PROMPT: The Hollow kneeling in shallow swamp water, the Witness's enormous pale hand reaching down from the mist to
touch her forehead; a contract of glowing green script unrolls in the air between them, its lower half too faint to
read. Reflections in black water, fireflies, a sense of a promise not fully understood.

prelude-hollow-4.webp
  "He had your hand the night you died. Something took it from him and sent you back..."
PROMPT: The Reaper standing in the swamp at night holding an empty hand out in front of him, fingers still curled as
if around another hand that has just been pulled away. Before him, the Hollow's soul-light is being drawn backward
into her body by threads of pale light from the mist. His red eyes wide, the first time he has been surprised.

prelude-hollow-5.webp
  "It begins in the marsh where you fell."
PROMPT: Wide view of the Drowned Wilds at night: endless flooded forest, twisted trees, green marsh lights reflected
in still black water, a broken healer's house on stilts. The Hollow walks away from us across the water's surface,
each step leaving a ripple of green light. Quiet, haunting.

THE ARCHITECT - "The Manufactured God"   (deep blue)
=====================================================

prelude-architect-1.webp
  "You are a king's son with no claim, kept out of sight all your life..."
PROMPT: A hidden study high in a noble house, cluttered with brass instruments, relic fragments in glass jars,
blueprints and diagrams of strange machines. The Architect in the deep blue hooded cloak stands at a drafting table,
several enchanted blades floating in a slow circle around him, his ice-blue eyes on a diagram. Candlelight, ink, a
narrow window onto a royal palace he cannot enter.

prelude-architect-2.webp
  "You will make your enemy yourself: a mind built from fragments of old powers and dead men's memories..."
PROMPT: A half-built construct on a workshop frame: a tall humanoid figure of dark iron, glass and floating relic
fragments, its chest an open cage holding swirling captured memory-light. It has no face yet, only a smooth blank
mask. Cables and runes connect it to the walls. Blue light, sparks, wonder and dread.

prelude-architect-3.webp
  "It will begin with no name and no self, and grow both. It will not want to serve you..."
PROMPT: The faceless construct, the Crownless King, sitting alone on a throne-like workbench in a dark workshop,
head tilted as if thinking, holding up one iron hand and studying it. Through a doorway behind it, the Architect is
seen small, asleep over his papers. A single blue light, eerie stillness.

prelude-architect-4.webp
  "He has read your mother's notes over your shoulder..."
PROMPT: The Architect reading an old leather notebook by candlelight, its pages full of machine diagrams and
handwritten notes. Directly behind his shoulder, unnoticed, the Reaper leans close and reads too, his red eyes on the
page. Intimate, quietly unsettling, deep blue shadows.

prelude-architect-5.webp
  "It begins the night your father's household is sold."
PROMPT: A noble house being stripped at night: servants and Crown clerks carrying out paintings, furniture and chests
by lantern light into waiting carts in the rain. The Architect watches from a dark upper window, blades floating
behind him, blueprints rolled under his arm. Cold rain, blue night, resolve.
