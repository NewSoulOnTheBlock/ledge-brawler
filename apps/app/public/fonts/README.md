# Typeface

The game's one typeface, used everywhere: the page (index.html, the design tokens' `sans` and `mono`), the canvas
(game/render.ts, the share card in App.tsx) and the privacy and terms pages (page.css). Kept here, at a fixed address,
so that the first paint does not wait on another site and the static pages can use it too.

- Evil Empire by Tup Wanders (https://wanders.work), one weight. SIL Open Font License 1.1: `evil-empire-OFL.txt`.
  Converted from the distributed OTF to WOFF2 with fontTools, glyphs unchanged.
