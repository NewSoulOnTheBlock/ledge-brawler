Privacy policy

Five Blades · in effect from 15 November 2026

Five Blades is a free game played in a web browser and in its Android app. This page says what the game keeps about the people who play it, why, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. If anything here is unclear, write to CONTACT_EMAIL.

Playing without signing in

  • On your device only: your progress in the campaigns, your settings (volume, controls, whether you have been shown the controls) and your best ranks are kept in your browser's storage on your own device. They are not sent to us. Clearing the site's data, or uninstalling the app, deletes them.
  • The leaderboards: if you put a name on the Survival or Daily leaderboard, we keep that name, the wave you reached, your knockouts, the fighter you played and when, and show them to every player. Choose a name that does not identify you. Names that are offensive are shown as "Player".
  • Reports: if you report a name on a leaderboard, we keep which entry you reported and a one-way code made from your internet address, so that one person cannot report the same entry twice. We never keep the address itself.

Signing in

Signing in is optional. It keeps your progress in the cloud so that it follows you between devices. Sign-in is provided by Privy, with an email address, a Google account or an X account. Privy handles the sign-in and gives us an account identifier, and the email address you signed in with, which is shown to you in the game. Privy may also create a wallet for your account; the game does not use it to hold or move anything. With your account identifier we keep your saved progress and the leaderboard entries you made while signed in.

Playing against others (Versus)

Online duels run through Multiplayer Agent. While you are in a duel, the name the game gives you (such as "Ember-123"), your fighter, and where your fighter is and what it is doing are sent to the other players in the same room. None of it is kept after the room closes. Multiplayer Agent also records anonymous usage figures (a random identifier for the device, the page, the language and time zone set on the device) and reports of errors in the game, so that problems can be found and fixed.

Hosting

The game is served by Vercel, which, like any web server, handles your internet address to deliver the game, and keeps short-lived logs. The leaderboards and saved progress are stored with Neon.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising, and we do not sell, rent or share what we keep with anyone for marketing.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or third-party trackers.
  • We do not ask for or keep your location, contacts, photos or anything else on your device.

Children

Five Blades is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep anything about them. If you believe a child has signed in or put personal information on a leaderboard, write to us and we will delete it.

Deleting what is kept

  • Signed in: open the menu under your name on the title screen and choose Delete my account. It deletes your saved progress, every leaderboard entry made while signed in, and your sign-in account, at once.
  • Not signed in: to have a leaderboard entry removed, write to us with the name and roughly when you played.
  • On your device: clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app.

How long things are kept

Leaderboard entries and saved progress are kept until you delete them or ask us to. Reports are kept until the entry they are about has been looked at.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with the date it took effect.
